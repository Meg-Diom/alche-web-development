Frequently Asked Questions

What is Avatar: The Last Airbender about?

It follows Aang, the last Airbender and Avatar, on his journey to master the four elements and bring balance to the world.

How many seasons does the series have?

The animated series has three seasons and a total of 61 episodes.

Who are Aang's closest friends?

His friends include Katara, Sokka, Toph, Zuko and later other allies who join their journey

Who created the series?

The series was created by Michael Dante DiMartino and Bryan Konietzko.
